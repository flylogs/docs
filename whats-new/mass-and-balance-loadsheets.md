# New: Mass & Balance loadsheet for every flight

**September 2026** · Aircraft & Flights

Flylogs now calculates a Mass & Balance loadsheet for each flight. Enter who and what is on board and the fuel, and the zero fuel, ramp, take-off and landing mass and centre of gravity are worked out and checked against the aircraft's limits.

***

### What changed

* **Set up a profile per aircraft** once: basic empty mass, arms, limits and the CG envelope, exactly as the flight manual gives them.
* **Loadsheet for each flight**, with the result plotted on the CG envelope.
* **Ramp and taxi fuel** are included, and a separate landing envelope can be set for aircraft with different landing limits.
* **Sign and export.** Loadsheets can be signed and exported as PDF by the people allowed to use them.

Each aircraft needs a profile before its flights can have a loadsheet.
