# New: class work and homework with grading

**September 2026** · Training courses

Teachers can now set homework for a class and grade it in Flylogs. Students see the brief and deadline, upload their work, and get the grade and feedback back, all in one place.

***

### What changed

* **Request class work** from the class page's **Classroom** tab. Set a deadline and write what students need to do. Students are notified and see a banner on the class page until they have dealt with it.
* **Students upload their work** before the deadline. They never see another student's file.
* **Grade it** with a score and a comment. The student is notified when the grade is in.
* **Submission status** is tracked for you: **On time**, **Late** or **Not submitted**.
* The student's **Homework** tab and the training report, including the PDF, list every class work item with its status, score and average.

***
