# New: Missed-class follow-up

**August 2026** · Training courses

Onsite classes now track why a student missed one, not just that they did. Attendance moves from a single tick box to four statuses, a missed-class email goes out automatically, and students can justify an absence or catch up with class work — all from the class page.

***

### Why we built it

A tick box only ever answered "were they there?". It couldn't say whether an absence was excused, whether the student later made up the missed content, or what a teacher expected them to do about it — so that follow-up happened by email and memory, if it happened at all. This puts the whole loop on the record: the student is notified the moment they're marked absent, they have a clear way to explain themselves or catch up, and a teacher or training manager decides — with every step kept for the training report.

***

### How it works

* **Four attendance statuses.** Each student is **Attended**, **Attended (post-class)**, **Absent**, or **Absent (justified)**, instead of unmarked. Nothing is recorded until the teacher **signs** the class — that's the moment every still-unmarked student becomes **Absent**.
* **Signing is repeatable.** A training manager can reopen a signed register, correct it, and sign again — every signature is kept as history, nothing is overwritten.
* **Automatic missed-class email.** Signing sends each **Absent** student one email naming the class, date, location and content, with a link back to the class page — once per student per class, even across a re-sign.
* **Class work, requested after the fact.** From the class page's **Documents** tab, a teacher can request class work with a deadline and description. Once the register is signed the request is frozen, but students can still upload — that's the point.
* **Absence justification.** An absent student can submit an explanation or a document from the **Attendance** tab. The teacher or a training manager approves or rejects it, with an optional note; approving sets **Absent (justified)**.
* **Uploads are permanent.** Class work and justification files can't be deleted or moved by anyone once uploaded — they're the evidence behind the decision.
* **Unenrolled invitees are excluded.** Someone invited to a class without being enrolled in the training can open it, but no attendance or evaluation is ever recorded for them — they're left out of the email, the class work notification and the attendance ratio.

***

### Where it fits

Everything lives on the class page: statuses and signing in the **Attendance** tab, class work requests and submissions in **Documents**, justifications alongside attendance. The student's training record, the training report and its PDF now split missed sessions into **justified**, **not justified**, and **credited after class**.

***

### Turning it on

This is part of onsite class attendance and needs no separate setup — signing a class register is already part of the existing workflow described in [Teacher tools](../training-courses/teacher-tools.md).
