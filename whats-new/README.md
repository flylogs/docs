## September 2026

**[Flylogs reads your flight remarks](flight-remark-findings.md)** · Safety\
Remarks that never became a safety report are now screened for findings a safety manager should see.\
[Manual →](../sms/flight-remark-findings.md)

**[A first draft of your mission comments](draft-mission-comment.md)** · Flights & Training\
Create draft turns the exercise gradings you just entered into a prose mission comment you can edit.\
[Manual →](../flights/draft-mission-comment.md)

**[Student attendance signatures](student-attendance-signatures.md)** · Training courses\
Students countersign their own attendance for a theory class, the way they sign a flight debrief.\
[Manual →](../training-courses/teacher-tools.md)

**[Preview a certificate before you issue it, and put one right afterwards](certificate-preview-and-withdrawal.md)** · Training courses\
See a course completion certificate before it counts, correct one that went out wrong, and withdraw one without destroying the record.\
[Manual →](../training-courses/student-management.md#the-course-completion-certificate)

**[Reserve an aircraft for selected pilots](aircraft-reserved-for-selected-pilots.md)** · Schedules & Fleet\
An aircraft can name the pilots allowed to self-book it — open to those pilots, invisible to everyone else.\
[Manual →](../schedules/self-scheduling.md#reserving-one-aircraft-for-a-few-pilots)

**[Flylogs tells you when the next check is due](next-maintenance-forecast.md)** · Fleet & Maintenance\
Every aircraft carries an estimate of when its next maintenance falls due, worked out from its own flying history.\
[Manual →](../aircraft/aircraft-maintenance/next-maintenance-forecast.md)

**[Restrict a flight type to specific aircraft](flight-type-aircraft-restriction.md)** · Flights & Fleet\
Tie a flight type to the aircraft it actually belongs on, instead of offering every type on every aircraft in the fleet.\
[Manual →](../flights/flight-types.md#aircraft)
