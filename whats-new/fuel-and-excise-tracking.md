# New: fuel and excise tracking

**September 2026** · Billing

The new **Billing → Fuel & Excise** page records what you pay for fuel and works out how much excise duty you can reclaim, so the refund is not left on the table.

***

### What changed

* **Refuellings** are recorded per aircraft, with the price per litre and the amounts. Fuel that is already on the aircraft's record is filled in for you.
* **Pricing a whole invoice at once**, with net amount and VAT, so your total always matches the supplier's.
* **Duty rates** are kept in one place and used for every refuelling.
* **Import a fuel card statement.** Most fuel supplier CSV exports can be imported directly.
* **Reclaim the duty.** The page shows how much excise you can reclaim on qualifying flights.
* **Checks** compare each uplift with the aircraft's own history, and fuel bought against fuel billed, to catch errors early.
