# New: aircraft managers

**September 2026** · Aircraft

One person, or a few, can now be given full control of a single aircraft. They manage its record, maintenance, documents and bookings, without access to the rest of the fleet.

***

### What changed

* **Name the managers** from the aircraft's edit page, in the **Aircraft Managers** card. They belong to the aircraft, not to the user, so you can change them when the aircraft changes hands.
* **An aircraft manager** can work on that aircraft: its record, maintenance jobs, documents and bookings.
* **Company rights stay with company staff.** A manager's authority covers one aeroplane. It does not give them company settings, billing or other aircraft.
* **Checked on the server**, per aircraft. Hiding a button is not the only protection.
