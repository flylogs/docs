# New: entry requirements and official result reports for exam sittings

**September 2026** · Training courses

Exam sittings can now check that a student is eligible before they register, and produce an official result report when the sitting is over.

***

### What changed

* **Entry requirements** are conditions Flylogs checks before a student can register for a sitting.
* **Draft and publish**: a new sitting is saved as a Draft, which students never see, until you publish it.
* **Attempt history**: attempts are counted, and a manager can reset a student's attempt history when needed.
* **Results**: enter results for each registration, and attach the official result report.
* **Audit for your authority**: sitting figures can be exported for the authority.

Creating, publishing and entering results is for Chief Pilot and above.
