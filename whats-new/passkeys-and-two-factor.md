# New: sign in with a passkey

**October 2026** · Account & security

Your device's fingerprint, Face ID or screen lock can now sign you in to Flylogs. A passkey replaces typing your password every time, and it is harder to phish than a password.

***

### What changed

* **Sign in with a passkey** on the Sign In page, instead of entering your email and password.
* **Unlock with passkey** on the auto-lock screen, to resume your session without typing anything.
* **Two-factor onboarding** walks you through setting up 2FA and a passkey in one place.
* Sign-ins that look suspicious may be asked to pass a short human check before they continue.

Passkeys are optional. Your password and authenticator app keep working.

***

See [Account security → Passkeys](../first-steps/account-security.md#passkeys) for setup and the full reference.
